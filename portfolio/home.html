What is my approach as a photographer?

Comfort comes first.

I want to be the photographer you can trust with your story. Someone you can be yourselves with, so the real emotion has somewhere to go.

People often ask if it is okay that they have never done this before. Sure! You do not need camera experience to be yourselves. The beautiful frames are the ones where you forget the lens and simply enjoy the moment.

Of course I will guide you through posing, but only as it feels comfortable and natural. That is where the best photographs live.

FAQ

Do I need to know how to pose?

No. I help with posing the whole time, so you don’t have to be afraid of the camera. Almost everyone in the photos on this site is not an actor or a model.

Edited photos only

You’ll receive all the best edited photos, carefully selected by me.

Studio rental

Studio rental is not included ($50–100/hour depending on location). I can help you choose and book one.

Sharing photos

I may use selected photos for my portfolio and social media. If you prefer privacy, just tell me before the shoot.

Styling & guidance

I help with posing, outfits, and overall direction so you feel comfortable even without experience.

Deposit

A deposit is required to secure your date. It is non-refundable, but we can reschedule your session if you give at least 24 hours’ notice.

Running late

If you arrive late, we can sometimes extend the session. Extra time is billed separately.
